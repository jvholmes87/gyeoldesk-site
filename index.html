Gyeoldesk 결데스크

The project desk for Korean contractors building on USFK installations. Task orders, proposals, submittals, permits, warranties and the weekly report, in Korean and English, in one place.

주한미군 기지 안에서 공사를 수행하는 한국 건설사를 위한 프로젝트 데스크. 과업, 입찰, 제출물, 허가, 하자보수, 주간 보고까지 한국어와 영어로 한곳에서 관리합니다.

What it covers

  • Projects by stage — Pre-NTP, construction, closeout — with installation, prime contractor and COR.
  • Submittal register, AF Form 3000, RFIs, dig permits (AF Form 103), TACW and RA, outage requests and JK-75 customs forms.
  • Weekly Action Report (WAR): a live weekly board that locks when final and prints as a landscape sheet.
  • Proposals and warranty requests, and project questions answered by email.
